I'm a data scientist and hobbyist security researcher based in Montréal. Professionally, I build ML pipelines for agricultural carbon quantification from satellite imagery and proximal sensing, geospatial workflows, and the unglamorous plumbing that makes model outputs trustworthy. On the side, I dig into network security, homelab infrastructure, and the security implications of generative AI.

My background spans chemical engineering, management, and data science, which mostly means I enjoy problems that sit between technical depth and strategy. Outside of work I read philosophy and think about technology's role in society — usually more skeptically than optimistically.
